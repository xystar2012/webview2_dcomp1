Composition Demo

This page is composited by DirectComposition over a native child window. The 648px circle in the middle is a real hole in this page - the moving GIF you see through it is drawn by the layer underneath, not by the browser.

Top layer - WebView2

Everything on the ring: this text, the cards and the gradient.

  • DComp target: BrowserWnd
  • Bounds mode: RAW_PIXELS
  • Rasterization scale: 1.0
  • Default background: {0,0,0,0}

Controls work anywhere, including over the cut-out: the page hit-tests the pointer itself and tells the host when a click belongs to the GIF instead.

Bottom layer - VideoWnd

An animated GIF on an ID2D1HwndRenderTarget, in a child HWND below this visual.

  • Frames: 110
  • Cadence: ~60 Hz
  • Drawn 1:1 across the cut-out

Because the WebView2 visual is transparent inside the circle, DirectComposition carries a round alpha edge and lets the GIF through - pixel for pixel.

Click test

Click a button to count it. Clicking the empty part of the circle instead pauses and resumes the GIF.
